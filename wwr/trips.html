Snake River

Winding through Southeast Idaho is the Snake River, one of our favorite whitewater rafting locations. It boasts many thrilling rapids with wide ranges of intensity, and is known as the "River of No Return". Perfect for adrenaline junkies! A trip down the Snake is sure to be an adventure you'll never forget.

Payette River

Nestled in the heart of Idaho’s Payette National Forest is a breathtaking destination for whitewater rafting. It's perfect for both seasoned adventurers and families seeking fun. Whether you’re tackling challenging drops or enjoying a scenic float, the Payette River guarantees an unforgettable experience!

Provo Canyon

Carving its way through the gorgeous landscapes of Utah is the Provo Canyon River. This is our favorite trip for families and beginners looking to get an introduction to the river. The canyon gives way to lush forest, guaranteeing a breathtaking rafting adventure you'll remember for years to come!
